What to Bring

Having these items ready ensures an efficient check-in and seamless clinical evaluation:

  • Photo Identification: Valid government-issued ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID).
  • Insurance Cards: Primary and secondary insurance cards, policy numbers, and subscriber details.
  • Medication Inventory: Current bottles or an exact list of all prescriptions, OTC drugs, vitamins, and dosages.
  • Prior Medical Records: Recent diagnostic reports, pathology notes, or imaging discs (CT/MRI) from outside systems.
  • Question Checklist: A written list of symptoms, timelines, and prioritized questions for your physician.

Insurance & Billing

Transparent, patient-focused billing and broad insurance network participation:

  • Major Networks Accepted: We participate in-network with Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare.
  • Referrals & Pre-Authorization: Some HMO and specialty plans mandate referrals. Our billing team assists in obtaining pre-authorization.
  • Copays & Deductibles: Any applicable copayments are collected during check-in via credit card, debit, HSA, or FSA accounts.
  • Self-Pay & Financial Aid: We offer upfront fee transparency, prompt-pay discounts, and income-based financial assistance programs.

Cancellation Policy

Our commitment to keeping clinical slots open for patients in need:

  • 24-Hour Notice: We respectfully request at least 24 hours advance notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your visit.
  • Convenient Rescheduling: Change your appointment date easily through your secure Patient Portal account or by calling our scheduling desk.
  • Illness Exceptions: Patients exhibiting sudden acute viral fever or respiratory emergencies are excused from cancellation timeframes.
  • Waitlist Activation: Timely cancellations enable us to offer urgent appointment openings to high-acuity patients waiting on our triage list.
Patient Guide

Preparing for Your Visit

Review these practical tips and guidelines to ensure a comfortable, well-organized, and productive clinical appointment.

Thorough preparation helps your clinical team make the most accurate assessment during your allotted consultation time:

  • Write Down Symptoms & Triggers: Keep a 7-day diary of when symptoms occur, severity on a scale of 1 to 10, activities that aggravate or alleviate them, and any family medical history of similar conditions.
  • Gather Prior Diagnostic Records: Request diagnostic imaging films (MRI/CT on CD-ROM or USB) and pathology reports from outside healthcare networks so our specialists can conduct side-by-side comparisons.
  • Observe Pre-Test Instructions: If your physician scheduled fasting laboratory work or pelvic imaging, adhere strictly to food and hydration guidelines. Drink only water during fasting hours unless advised otherwise.
  • Arrange Transportation if Sedated: If undergoing an outpatient endoscopic procedure, eye dilation, or minor surgical intervention, arrange for an adult companion to drive you home safely.

Navigating the day of your clinic appointment with ease:

  • Recommended Arrival Time: Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment (30 minutes early for new patient intake) to verify contact information and complete vital signs triage.
  • Express Self Check-In: Use our touchscreen kiosks in the lobby or complete electronic check-in via your smartphone upon arrival to minimize waiting room time.
  • Comfortable Attire: Wear loose, comfortable, two-piece clothing that allows easy access for blood pressure cuffs, stethoscopes, physical range-of-motion assessments, or ECG electrode placement.
  • Companion Policy: You are welcome to bring a family member, trusted friend, or caregiver to accompany you into the examination room for support and note-taking.

Your appointment is an open dialogue where you are an active partner in your care:

  • Be Candid About Your Health: Share candid information regarding lifestyle, diet, alcohol intake, sleep, stress, and adherence to past medication regimens. Healthcare providers maintain strict confidentiality.
  • Key Questions to Ask:
    • "What do you believe is the underlying cause of my symptoms?"
    • "What are the benefits, risks, and side effects of this treatment or prescription?"
    • "Are there alternative non-pharmaceutical therapies or lifestyle adjustments I should consider?"
    • "When should I expect symptoms to improve, and what symptoms should prompt an immediate call?"
  • Clarify Treatment Steps: Before leaving the exam room, ask the doctor or clinical nurse to summarize next steps, diagnostic orders, and follow-up timelines in writing.

For patients scheduled for a virtual video consultation:

  • Pre-Session Device Test: Test your smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer camera, speaker, and microphone at least 15 minutes prior to your session. Ensure a stable high-speed Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
  • Private & Well-Lit Environment: Choose a quiet, well-lit room free of background television, pets, and interruptions. Face toward a light source so your clinician can visually examine skin tone, breathing, and visible symptoms.
  • Measure At-Home Vitals: If you possess a home blood pressure monitor, pulse oximeter, thermometer, or scale, record your current readings right before logging on to provide data to your provider.

Continuing your care trajectory smoothly after your appointment has concluded:

  • After-Visit Summary (AVS): Access your complete physician consultation notes, prescription changes, and diagnosis codes via the Patient Portal within 1 hour of checkout.
  • Rapid Lab & Imaging Results: Routine blood and urine tests post to your portal within 24 to 48 hours. Complex imaging reads (CT/MRI) and biopsies are typically finalized within 3 to 5 business days with clinician notes.
  • Prescription Dispatch: New electronic prescriptions are transmitted directly to your preferred retail or mail-order pharmacy. Contact your pharmacy directly for pickup alerts.
  • Direct Clinical Messaging: Have follow-up questions about medication dosages? Message your care team through the secure portal message center.
