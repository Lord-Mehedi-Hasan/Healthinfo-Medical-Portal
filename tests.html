Blood Test (CBC)

Complete Blood Count with Differential

Evaluates overall health and detects a wide variety of conditions including anemia, infection, leukemia, and clotting disorders by measuring red cells, white cells, hemoglobin, and platelets.

MRI

Magnetic Resonance Imaging

Uses a powerful magnetic field, radio frequency pulses, and specialized software to generate detailed cross-sectional pictures of soft tissues, brain, spinal cord, joints, and organs without radiation.

Duration: 30–60 mins
Prep: Remove all metallic objects

CT Scan

Computed Tomography (CAT Scan)

Combines a series of X-ray views taken from multiple angles with computer processing to create comprehensive cross-sectional images of bones, blood vessels, trauma injuries, and internal organs.

Duration: 10–20 mins
Prep: Contrast dye instructions

X-Ray

Diagnostic Radiography

A rapid, noninvasive imaging procedure that uses minimal doses of electromagnetic radiation to visualize bone fractures, joint dislocations, chest infections, pneumonia, and foreign objects.

Duration: 10–15 mins
Prep: Loose, metal-free clothing

Ultrasound

Diagnostic Sonography / Sonogram

Employs high-frequency sound waves to capture real-time dynamic images of heart valves, blood flow, abdominal organs, pelvic structures, and fetal development without ionizing radiation.

An endoscopic examination of the large intestine (colon) and rectum to detect changes, ulcers, inflamed tissue, and precancerous polyps. Both a preventive screening and biopsy procedure.

Records the electrical signals of the heart via sensors attached to the chest and extremities to detect arrhythmias, evaluate chest pain, identify past heart attacks, and assess cardiac health.

A specialized low-dose X-ray examination designed to detect early breast cancer, microcalcifications, cysts, and tumors years before physical lumps can be felt during clinical breast exams.

Duration: 15–20 mins
Prep: Avoid deodorant / lotions

Extraction of a microscopic sample of cells, fluid, or tissue from an abnormal lump or lesion for definitive pathology testing to distinguish between benign anomalies and malignant conditions.

Duration: 20–45 mins

Examines the lining of the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum with a flexible lighted tube containing an HD camera to identify ulcers, Barrett's esophagus, chronic GERD, and cause of bleeding.

Monitors heart rhythm, blood pressure, and breathing while you walk on a treadmill to reveal coronary artery disease, exercise-induced arrhythmias, and heart pumping adequacy under physical exertion.

Patient Preparation

How to Prepare for Your Diagnostic Exam

Following pre-exam instructions ensures safe procedures, clear diagnostic imaging, and highly reliable laboratory findings.

1

Verify Fasting Orders

Certain blood panels, abdominal ultrasounds, and endoscopies require 8–12 hours of fasting. Drink only plain water unless instructed otherwise.

2

Review Current Medications

Tell your care team about all prescriptions, blood thinners, insulin, or supplements. Some may need to be adjusted or paused prior to procedures.

3

Wear Metal-Free Clothing

For MRI, CT, and X-ray procedures, wear comfortable clothes free of metal zippers, snaps, or jewelry. You may be provided a sanitized clinical gown.

4

Track Digital Results

Most routine laboratory results post to your secure Patient Portal account within 24 to 48 hours, accompanied by physician interpretation notes.
