Respiratory

Asthma

A chronic pulmonary disorder where bronchial airways swell, produce excess mucus, and undergo bronchospasms, causing acute wheezing, chest tightness, and shortness of breath.

Pulmonology Learn More
Oncology

Breast Cancer

Malignant cellular transformation of breast ductal or lobular tissue. Screened with mammography and managed with targeted oncologic therapies, radiation, and surgical resection.

Surgical Oncology Learn More
Renal & Urinary

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

Gradual, irreversible decline in nephron filtration capability. Often secondary to long-standing diabetes or hypertension, leading to electrolyte imbalance and systemic toxicity.

Nephrology Learn More
Respiratory

COPD (Chronic Obstructive)

A cluster of progressive inflammatory pulmonary diseases (emphysema and chronic bronchitis) that impair alveolar gas exchange and obstruct airway airflow.

Pulmonology Learn More
Endocrine & Metabolic

Diabetes Type 2

A chronic metabolic disorder causing peripheral tissue insulin resistance and pancreatic beta-cell fatigue, causing hyperglycemia, fatigue, and vascular complications.

Brain & Nerves

Epilepsy

A chronic neurological condition marked by recurrent, unprovoked epileptic seizures resulting from abnormal, synchronized bursts of electrical activity in the cortex.

Neurology Learn More
Chronic Pain

Fibromyalgia

A central sensitization syndrome characterized by chronic, generalized musculoskeletal aches, tenderness, severe debilitating fatigue, sleep disturbance, and cognitive clouding.

Rheumatology Learn More
Heart & Vascular

Heart Failure (CHF)

A clinical syndrome resulting from structural or functional cardiac impairment that prevents the ventricles from filling with or ejecting adequate arterial blood volume.

Cardiology Learn More
Heart & Vascular

Hypertension

Persistently elevated systemic arterial blood pressure (≥130/80 mmHg). Often asymptomatic for years while accelerating atherosclerotic damage and coronary vessel injury.

Cardiology Learn More
Autoimmune

Lupus (SLE)

Systemic autoimmune pathology wherein circulating antinuclear antibodies trigger microvascular inflammation across joints, renal parenchyma, dermis, and serosal membranes.

Immunology Learn More
Brain & Nerves

Multiple Sclerosis (MS)

An inflammatory demyelinating disease of the central nervous system that disrupts axonal conduction, causing vision problems, paresis, ataxia, and sensory paresthesias.

Neurology Learn More
Musculoskeletal

Osteoarthritis

The most prevalent degenerative joint disease, causing gradual loss of hyaline articular cartilage, osteophyte formation, periarticular stiffness, and weight-bearing pain.

Orthopedics Learn More
Brain & Nerves

Parkinson's Disease

A slowly progressive movement disorder caused by apoptosis of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra, manifesting as resting tremors, rigidity, and postural instability.

Neurology Learn More
Autoimmune

Rheumatoid Arthritis

A chronic systemic autoimmune disease leading to persistent synovial pannus inflammation, progressive cartilage erosion, joint deformity, and extra-articular manifestations.

Rheumatology Learn More
Brain & Vascular

Stroke (CVA)

A critical neurologic emergency caused by cerebral thrombosis, embolism, or hemorrhage leading to rapid ischemic cell necrosis. Recognized by sudden facial droop, arm weakness, and dysarthria.
