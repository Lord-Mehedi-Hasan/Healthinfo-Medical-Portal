Visitor Services

Transit, Parking & Accessibility

We are committed to making your visit as stress-free as possible from the moment you arrive on our medical campuses.

Complimentary Parking

Covered ramps and climate-controlled skyways connect parking decks to clinic lobbies at Main Medical Center and West Campus. Valet available at main entrance.

Campus Shuttle Service

Free electric campus shuttles run every 10 minutes between Main Medical Center, West Campus, and surrounding parking facilities Monday through Friday.

Full Accessibility Support

All facilities are 100% ADA compliant with automated entrances, oversized elevators, wheelchair assistance stations at entrances, and tactile signage.

Language & Interpreters

Onsite medical interpreters in Spanish, Somali, Hmong, and American Sign Language (ASL) are provided free of charge, along with 24/7 video interpretation.
