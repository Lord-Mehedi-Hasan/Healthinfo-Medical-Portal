Patient Assistance

Frequently Asked Health Questions

Essential information regarding emergency care, appointments, and how to verify medical research.

Emergency Department (ED): Seek emergency care immediately for life-threatening conditions such as chest pressure, sudden numbness or speech difficulty (stroke symptoms), severe uncontrollable bleeding, head trauma with loss of consciousness, or extreme shortness of breath.

Urgent Care Clinic: Visit urgent care for non-life-threatening ailments requiring same-day evaluation, including minor sprains, mild asthma flares, standard fever, minor cuts requiring stitches, ear infections, and urinary tract infections.

To maximize your consultation, bring an up-to-date printed list of all prescription medications, vitamins, and supplements with dosages. Write down your symptom timeline (when they started, what triggers or alleviates them), and compile your top three questions for the physician.

Request copies of previous lab work and relevant imaging discs beforehand, or confirm they have been transferred to the receiving clinic.
