Drug Interaction Checker

Simulate and examine potential pharmacokinetic interactions between two medications, over-the-counter products, or nutritional supplements before co-administration.

Moderate Interaction
Lisinopril + Ibuprofen

Co-administration of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as Ibuprofen may decrease the antihypertensive efficacy of ACE inhibitors like Lisinopril and increase the risk of deterioration in renal function, particularly in elderly or volume-depleted patients.

Clinical Guidance: Monitor blood pressure regularly and consult your healthcare provider or pharmacist. Your physician may recommend alternative pain relief options such as Acetaminophen.

Safe Medication Practices

Best Practices for Managing Your Medications

Collaborating closely with your physician and clinical pharmacist ensures optimal therapeutic outcomes while minimizing preventable adverse reactions.

Maintain an Up-to-Date List

Keep a comprehensive record of all medications, including OTC remedies, vitamins, and herbal extracts with dosages. Present it at every medical appointment.

Proper Medication Storage

Store medications in a cool, dry place away from heat and moisture (avoid humid bathroom medicine cabinets). Keep all bottles locked and away from children.

Never Share Prescriptions

Prescriptions are formulated for your specific weight, age, kidney function, and clinical condition. Never share medication or alter dosages without doctor approval.

Safe Drug Disposal

Safely discard expired or unused drugs using pharmacy take-back kiosks or community drug disposal programs rather than flushing them down household plumbing.

Need a Medication Review or Clinical Consultation?

Schedule a dedicated pharmacotherapy consultation with our outpatient physicians and specialists to review your regimen and optimize your wellness.
